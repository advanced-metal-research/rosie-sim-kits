Rosie 1000 simulation and CAD kit
Copyright 2026 Advanced Metal Research. https://advancedmetalresearch.com

Free to use for simulation and integration. You may use, copy, modify and share
these files, including in commercial work, to simulate, program, plan and
integrate Rosie robots and the cells they work in.

The geometry is the robot's real outside surfaces: each link is one solid,
filled inside, with the structural parts' own CAD faces and the motors and
gearboxes as plain envelopes of the same size.
